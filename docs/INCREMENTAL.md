# INCREMENTAL.md

What body pose adds to the XY baseline for the outcome of an on-ball engagement. Phase 4 of docs/PLAN.md.
Design fixed in docs/PREREGISTRATION.md before any pose feature was computed. Status: done on 11 November 2026.

**Headline.** On 1,875 on-ball engagements of two matches, body orientation from broadcast pose adds no detectable explanatory
value for possession loss beyond XY tracking: the out-of-sample log loss changes by minus 0.0002 under both bounds of the
angular uncertainty (95% intervals within minus 0.0004 to 0.0000). Carriers pressed from outside their visual field lose the ball
in 13.2% of engagements against 10.8% when the presser is in view (head direction, lower bound. Upper bound: 12.1% against 10.6%).
The difference of plus 2.4 points (minus 1.2 to plus 5.8) shrinks to plus 1.1 (minus 2.2 to plus 4.4) once XY context is accounted for.

This is a null result. It is reported as pre-registered. The blind-side metric is implemented and geometrically verified.
It is not validated as an explanatory variable.

### 1.1 Sources and definitions

- Design: docs/PREREGISTRATION.md, sections 3 to 11 and the additions of 11 November 2026 in section 12. All numbers in this block are produced by `scripts/incremental_value.py`.
- Outcome: possession loss of the pressed possession. Baseline logit `z_xy`: the frozen XY baseline of docs/BASELINE.md, refitted here and checked against the Phase 3 cache.
- Pose features: `p_blind_side_head`, `p_blind_side_torso`, `presser_alignment`, `carrier_openness` (`blindside.features.pose_features`), field of view half-angle 100 degrees, 200 Monte Carlo draws, sigma = p90 / 2.5, seed 0.
- Bounds: `lower` uses the left/right error correlation at which the Monte Carlo SD equals the frame-to-frame jitter, `upper` is independence (docs/RELIABILITY.md section 1).
- Models: Model 0 = outcome on `z_xy`. Model 1 = Model 0 plus the four pose features, L2 penalty on the pose coefficients only, chosen inside each training fold (5-fold, grouped by possession).
- Out-of-fold predictions: grouped 10-fold cross-validation, groups = possessions, stratified by match, 20 repeats, predictions averaged.
- Intervals: paired cluster bootstrap over possessions, 2,000 resamples, stratified by match, percentile 95%, on the fixed out-of-fold predictions. They cover the sampling of possessions, not the variability of refitting.

### 1.10 Exploratory, not pre-registered: XY proxy against the pose blind-side label

How well does XY tracking alone reproduce the pose-based blind-side label? No outcome column is used in this section.

- XY proxy: blind if `approach_angle_heading_deg` exceeds 100 degrees, the angle between the carrier's direction of travel over the 0.5 s before t0 and the direction to the presser. Same threshold as the field of view half-angle.
- Pose label, rows by speed and the row `all`: blind if `p_blind_side` exceeds 0.5, on every included engagement. Row `all, uncertain bin dropped`: the pre-registered bins, blind above 0.9 against in view below 0.1, without the engagements between.
- Agreement: share of engagements with the same label. Kappa: Cohen's kappa, agreement beyond what the two blind rates give by chance. Recall: share of the pose-blind engagements that the proxy also calls blind. Precision: share of the proxy-blind engagements that the pose label also calls blind.

## 2. Reading of the results

Measured, pre-registered:
- **H1 is not supported.** Model 1 does not beat Model 0 under either bound (1.4). The decision rule of section 9 gives
  "no detectable increment". ROC AUC, Brier score and calibration slope agree.
- **The interval below zero does not mean that pose harms.** The inner cross-validation picks the strongest shrinkage on offer
  (median `C` 0.001, the lower end of the grid), so Model 1 is Model 0 plus four coefficients near zero (1.5).
  The difference of 0.0002 is the cost of estimating them. It is 0.4% of what the XY baseline gains over the constant
  prediction (0.050, docs/BASELINE.md).
- **No robustness check changes the conclusion.** None of the ten checks has an interval above zero, under either bound (1.7):
  not the usability subset, not the weaker baselines without the SkillCorner labels (check 3) or without the phase type
  as well (check 9), not slow carriers (check 10), where pose had the best chance because the direction of travel says
  the least about where the carrier faces.
- **H2 has the hypothesised direction and is not distinguishable from zero.** Possession loss is more frequent in the blind bin
  than in the in-view bin for head and torso under both bounds, by 1.6 to 2.4 points, with every interval including zero
  (1.6, contrast in 1.9). After the XY baseline, 0.2 to 1.2 points remain.
- **The error model changes who counts as blind, not the conclusion.** Under the lower bound 3.0% of engagements fall into
  the uncertain bin of `p_blind_side_head` and 357 are blind. Under the upper bound 18.2% are uncertain and 247 are blind (1.3).
  The bounds agree on the bin for 84.7% of engagements. If the joint errors are independent, about one engagement in six
  cannot be called blind or in view, and a point estimate without uncertainty would hide that.
- **Secondary outcomes show the same.** No increment for wide turnover or forced backward, and no gradient of
  `possession_epv_total` across the bins (1.8).
- **Swapped labels are rare.** 3 carrier head poses, 1 carrier torso pose and 4 presser hip poses of 1,875 are flagged (1.2).

Exploratory, not pre-registered (1.9):
- Without any penalty the pose features make the out-of-sample log loss worse by 0.002, with the interval below zero.
  The null result is not produced by the shrinkage.
- The unpenalised coefficient of `p_blind_side_head` is plus 0.11 per standard deviation under the lower bound, with an interval
  from minus 0.18 to plus 0.39 (odds ratio 0.84 to 1.47). That interval is the resolution of this data set: an effect of that
  size or smaller cannot be told from zero with 217 events.
- XY already knows part of it. `p_blind_side` correlates with the baseline's angle between the carrier's direction of travel
  and the direction to the presser (Spearman 0.35 to 0.42).
- The highest loss rates are in the uncertain bin, where the presser stands near the edge of the visual field
  (head, lower bound: 21.4% of 56 engagements, 7.4 points above the XY expectation, interval minus 2.1 to plus 19.0).
  The interval includes zero and the bin was not singled out beforehand. It is a lead, not a finding.

Exploratory, not pre-registered, no outcome used (1.10):
- **The XY proxy reproduces the pose label poorly overall.** Calling an engagement blind when the presser is more than
  100 degrees off the carrier's direction of travel agrees with the head label in 65.8% of engagements (63.5 to 68.0),
  Cohen's kappa 0.28 (0.24 to 0.31). For the torso label kappa is 0.35 (0.31 to 0.39).
- **The proxy calls far more engagements blind than pose does.** 45.5% against 20.5% (head). It finds 77% of the pose-blind
  engagements, and 35% of the engagements it calls blind are blind by pose. As a stand-in for the blind side it over-calls
  by a factor of about two.
- **Agreement grows with carrier speed, as expected before the run.** Head label: kappa 0.09 (0.04 to 0.15) below 2 m/s,
  0.31 at 2 to 4, 0.54 at 4 to 6 and 0.58 (0.41 to 0.74) above 6 m/s. Torso label: 0.10, 0.41, 0.66 and 0.77 (0.63 to 0.90).
  Below 2 m/s the proxy is close to chance, and 665 of the 1,875 engagements (35%) are in that band. The direction of travel
  of a slow carrier says little about where they face (docs/RELIABILITY.md section 2.4).
- **The head follows the direction of travel less than the torso at every speed above 2 m/s.** At 4 m/s and more, 57 to 58%
  of the proxy-blind engagements are blind by the head label against 68 to 81% by the torso label. Whether that gap is
  head turning or head noise cannot be told here, the head has the larger angular uncertainty.
- **The bounds do not matter for this comparison.** A label cut at `p_blind_side` 0.5 is almost the point estimate, so both
  bounds give the same table to within 0.2 points. They differ only in the row without the uncertain bin, which drops
  56 engagements under the lower bound and 342 under the upper one (head), and kappa stays at 0.29 to 0.30.
- **Two readings, not separable without ground truth.** Low agreement means either that pose carries orientation that XY
  does not have, or that one of the two labels is noisy. Kappa measures agreement, not which label is right.
  Together with 1.4 it also says: the information is different, and it still does not improve the prediction of possession loss.

What this does not show:
- That body orientation is irrelevant for pressing. It shows that one orientation snapshot at the engagement start does not
  improve the prediction of possession loss in two matches, on top of a baseline that already has positions, speeds,
  the approach angle to the direction of travel and SkillCorner's pressure label.
- That the carrier was unaware of the presser. `p_blind_side` describes the visual field at t0. A carrier who scanned
  half a second earlier counts as blind. This is an interpretation, not a measurement.
- Anything about matches other than these two. The intervals describe the sampling of possessions inside them.

Assumptions, labelled:
- Visual field half-angle of 100 degrees around the head or torso direction (check 4 varies it).
- Isotropic Gaussian joint error with sigma = p90 / 2.5 (check 5), left/right correlation between the two bounds.
- Positions are exact. The eyes are not tracked, the ear axis stands in for gaze.
- Possession loss is a property of the possession. It also happens for reasons unrelated to this presser.

Status of deliverable 3, in the terms of CLAUDE.md:
- **Implemented and validated:** the geometry of the four pose features. Unit tests on synthetic skeletons, and the carrier's
  facing angles agree with the Phase 1 orientation table to within 0.01 degrees for every included engagement
  (checked on every run of the script).
- **Implemented, not validated:** `p_blind_side` as a variable that explains engagement outcomes. The pre-registered test
  found no increment.
- **Proposed:** orientation over the approach (the second before t0) instead of one frame, and a test on more matches
  once more body pose is published.
